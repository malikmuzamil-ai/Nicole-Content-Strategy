# 01 · How This System Works

One page. If somebody reads nothing else before starting, this is the page.

---

## The operating model in one sentence

Nicole talks for forty minutes a week, the desk turns that into three posts and roughly twelve
comments, she approves every word, and the only outcome we measure is how many of the right people
apply for an Assess.

---

## The loop

```
  MONDAY            Brain dump call, 30 to 40 minutes. Her voice, raw.
     |
     v
  TUESDAY           Desk drafts three posts against the calendar and the pillar weights.
     |
     v
  WEDNESDAY         Quality gate. Fourteen points. Failures are rewrites, not edits.
     |
     v
  THURSDAY          Batch to Nicole. She approves, cuts, or sends back with one word.
     |
     v
  MON / TUE / THU   Publish. The 30/30 window runs around each post.
     |
     v
  FRIDAY            Weekly report. Assess applications first, everything else second.
     |
     v
  back to Monday
```

Her publishing days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Three posts a week is the **ceiling**, not a
floor to defend by cutting corners.

---

## Who does what

| Role | Held by | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal** | Nicole | The final yes. Every post, every comment, every DM. The Assess call itself |
| **Desk** | Purely Personal | Drafting, the window, the log, the report, the calendar |
| **Design** | Purely Personal | Cards, carousels, cheatsheets, from a written brief only |
| **Nobody** | | Publishing without her yes. There is no such role |

---

## The four things that cannot be delegated away

1. **Her approval.** No standing green lane exists yet. Until she grants one, every comment is
   drafted, batched and held.
2. **The Assess call.** Content brings in four clients, not four hundred applicants. One person
   still has to be in the room after.
3. **Her objects and her stories.** If a post needs a physical detail she has not given us, we ask
   her for one. We do not invent it.
4. **The confidentiality line.** No client detail, no unconfirmed credential, no exception for a
   stronger story.

---

## What content is actually for

It is not the goal. It is the unpaid channel that turns a stranger into an application.

Ninety days, thirty six posts, twelve Assess sessions, four private clients. That is the whole
machine. Everything in this folder exists to make those four numbers happen in that order.

---

## The one test that overrides the others

> If it sounds like a woman who has been in the room and has nothing left to prove, it is right.

If you cannot hear her saying it across a table, it is wrong. Read every draft aloud before it
goes anywhere.
