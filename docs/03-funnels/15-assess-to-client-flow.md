## The application form

Short. Seven questions. It is doing the qualifying work described in doc 02, and it is the first
thing that tells the applicant this is not a free consultation.

1. What do you own, or control
2. What decision is in front of you in the next six months
3. Who else knows the whole of it
4. What have you already tried
5. **Is a genuinely different future available to you, or only to other people**
6. What would have to be true in six months for this to have been worth it
7. Anything you have not said to anyone

**Question 5 is the belief test** and it is the single most predictive answer on the form. Question
7 is the one that tells us whether test three in doc 02 is met. An applicant who leaves 7 blank is
not disqualified, but the pattern is worth watching.

**No budget question.** The price is stated on the page above the form. Somebody who applies has
seen it.

---

## Screening

The desk screens and recommends. **Nicole decides.** Every time, including the obvious yes.

| Signal | Read |
|---|---|
| Owns or controls, decision inside six months, answers 5 and 7 honestly | Strong yes |
| Everything true except the six month decision | Hold. Revisit when the decision is real. The urgency is the qualifier |
| Wants a framework, or asks whether it is coaching | Decline. Doc 06 |
| Answers 5 with "only incremental progress is real" | **Decline.** This is the one she turns away |
| Wants it for somebody else, a spouse, a co-founder, a successor | Decline as framed. Offer nothing else |

---

## The decline

Sent by Nicole, under sixty words, no explanation of the criteria, no referral, no "when you are
ready". A decline that argues its case invites a negotiation.

**Draft**
> Thank you for writing, and for answering the questions properly. This is not the right piece of
> work for what is in front of you. I would rather say that now than halfway through.
>
> I hope it goes well.

No door left open, because a door left open is a decline that is not a decline.

---

## The Assess

Half a day. Private. CHF 4,500, paid before the date.

**What she is doing in it.** Mapping the situation and establishing whether the six month work
makes sense. It is a real piece of work with standalone value, not a sales call.

**What the client leaves with.** The map. Whether or not she continues.

**What does not happen in it.** A pitch. A proposal presented at the end. A next step assumed. If
the six months is right, she says so and the proposal follows separately.

---

## The proposal

One page. Sent within 48 hours of the Assess.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| What we established | Three or four lines. Her situation, in her own words where possible |
| What the six months is | What's Next, Done Right. The five acts, named, not explained |
| What it costs | CHF 18,000. 6,000 to begin, then four payments of 3,000 |
| When it starts | A date |

No testimonials. No logos. No comparison. No bonus. No deadline on the price. A deadline at this
altitude reads as a tactic, and it is.

---

## Follow up

**Once, after ten days, from Nicole.** Under forty words.

> I have kept the start date open. Let me know either way and I will take it out of the diary.

Then nothing. No sequence, no nurture, no re-engagement campaign. Somebody who has sat through a
half day and read a one page proposal has all the information. Repeated contact tells them the
practice is not full.

**The stated reason on a decline is the most valuable single data point in the funnel.** Record it
verbatim. It is the only place the price decision, O1, will be answered by evidence rather than by
opinion.
