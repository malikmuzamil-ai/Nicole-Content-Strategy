# 13 · Funnel Architecture

**Status:** SETTLED. The maths holds at the current price. One live decision, O1.
**The principle:** almost none of the content sells directly. The offer surfaces in a few earned
places, and the whole system is judged against one number.

---

## The five steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1 · Post** | Fits one pillar. No outside links. Hook in the first two lines |
| **2 · Signal** | Comments and saves show who is ready to go further |
| **3 · Application** | The Assess link lives in the Featured section and the bio, **never inside the post itself** |
| **4 · Resolution** | The Assess maps the situation and shows whether the six month work makes sense |
| **5 · What's Next, Done Right** | The right clients move into the six month private engagement. **That is where this whole funnel is aimed** |

---

## The rule that governs step 1

**A Selling post never runs without two Authority or Education posts directly ahead of it.**

The Assess is always earned, never a cold ask. In practice this means a maximum of one Selling
intent post per week from month two, and zero in month one.

---

## Where the offer may appear

Three places. Listed again here because this is the rule most likely to erode quietly.

1. The **Featured section** of her profile
2. The **bio link**
3. **Her own first comment** on a Selling intent post that has earned it

Never in a post body. Never in a comment on somebody else's post. Never in a DM that she has not
personally approved.

---

## The numbers, worked backward

**Four clients at the settled price is the ninety day target.** That is the bar content has to
clear, not the other way round.

| | |
|---|---|
| **4** | private clients, first 90 days |
| **CHF 72,000** | at the current settled price |
| **~12** | Assess sessions likely needed |
| **36** | posts published across the same window |

Twelve Assess sessions at CHF 4,500 is a further CHF 54,000, which is real revenue rather than a
cost of sale. The Assess pays for itself and funds the acquisition of the six month work.

**The Assess to client figure assumes roughly one in three converts. A working guess, not a
researched fact, and it should be corrected the moment real numbers come in.**

---

## The chain, with the assumptions named

Each of these is an assumption, and each one should be replaced with a measured number as soon as
there is one. Naming them as assumptions is what makes them correctable.

| Stage | Assumption | Ninety day figure |
|---|---|---|
| Posts published | Settled | 36 |
| Profile visits per post | Unknown. **Measure from week one** | to be measured |
| Applications per profile visit | Unknown. **Measure from week one** | to be measured |
| Applications | Derived target | ~18 to 20 |
| Application to Assess booked | Assumed 2 in 3, after the belief test screens | 12 |
| Assess to six month client | **Assumed 1 in 3. A guess** | 4 |

The two unknowns in the middle are the only genuinely unmeasured part of the funnel. Both are
visible in LinkedIn analytics plus the application form from day one. Until they are measured,
nobody should claim the funnel is working or failing.

---

## The minimum bars

These are diagnostics. Missing one for two consecutive weeks triggers a review, not a panic.

| Signal | Bar |
|---|---|
| **Save rate** | Saves at least half of likes on Carousel and Cheatsheet posts |
| **Comment depth** | 8 or more real comments per Teach Me post |
| **Consistency** | 3 times a week, no weeks skipped |
| **Assess applications** | Tracked weekly from day one. **The only number that actually matters** |

A week where every bar is missed and two applications arrive is a good week. A week where every
bar is cleared and nothing arrives is a diagnostic, not a success.

---

## What we do not do, and why

| Not doing | Why |
|---|---|
| A lead magnet | The download audience is not this audience, and it fills the list with the wrong people |
| A comment keyword trigger | Engagement bait with a respectable face. It also breaks the DM rule |
| Paid amplification | Reach is not the mechanism. Consequence is |
| A newsletter, for now | Not until the posting rhythm has run clean for a quarter |
| Booking links in posts | The offer is earned at the profile, which is where the qualification happens |
| Automated outreach of any kind | No DM goes out under her name before her approval |

---

## What happens when the funnel underperforms

In order. Do not skip to the bottom.

1. **Check consistency first.** Three a week, no weeks skipped. Most funnel problems are
   attendance problems
2. **Check the profile,** doc 14. A post drives to a profile. If the profile has not been
   rewritten, the funnel has a hole at exactly the point of highest intent
3. **Check pillar balance.** A month heavy on Make Me Think generates reaction and no applications
   by design. Rebalance toward Teach Me and Understand Me
4. **Check comment depth on Teach Me posts.** That is the earliest honest signal of whether the
   right people are reading
5. **Only then** question the offer or the price

**Never** respond to a slow month by posting more often. Three a week is the ceiling. Volume is
watchpoint 01 and it is the fastest way to lose the position that makes the whole thing work.
