# 14 · Profile Conversion Assets

**Status:** DRAFT. **Every word here needs her sign off before it goes live.** Open decision O4.
**Why this is urgent:** every post drives to the profile. If the profile has not been rewritten,
the funnel has a hole at exactly the point of highest intent. This should be done in week one,
before the volume arrives.

---

## What the profile has to do

A reader arrives having just read one card. She has thirty seconds and one question: **is this
person actually who that post implied.**

The profile answers it, states what is available, and stops. It does not sell, it does not list
achievements, and it does not explain the method.

---

## Headline

220 characters. The most read line she owns.

**Draft A, the plain one**
> Advisor to founders and owner-CEOs. What's Next, Done Right.

**Draft B, the one that carries the thesis**
> I work with people who have achieved everything except themselves. Advisor to founders and owner-CEOs.

**Draft C, the quietest**
> Advisor. Twenty five years around people with considerable power.

**Recommendation: B.** It is the only one that does the qualifying work in the headline itself, and
it uses her own sentence, which is the strongest asset we have. A is safe and says nothing. C is
beautiful and leaves the reader unsure what is on offer.

**Banned in the headline:** coach, mentor, speaker, author, any emoji, any pipe-separated list of
nouns, any "helping X do Y" construction, any follower count or award.

---

## About section

2,600 characters available. Use roughly 900. The first 250 characters show before the fold, so the
first two lines carry the whole weight.

**Draft**

> They have achieved everything. They have not achieved themselves.
>
> That sentence describes most of the people I work with. They own the business. The decision
> stops with them. From the outside there is nothing to fix, which is exactly what makes it
> impossible to explain to anyone who could fix a broken thing.
>
> I am not a coach. A coach hands the question back. I am not a consultant, and I do not sell
> frameworks. I am not a therapist, and I do not work through the past. I work forwards and
> upwards.
>
> What I build with somebody is a life operating system rather than a vision. Anyone can produce a
> vision in a workshop. Almost nobody survives the first real pressure test after.
>
> Twenty five years around people with considerable power. A crisis response in Halifax. A
> turnaround inside a Swiss private bank. Years studying Taoism, and training in existential
> psychology. Positive psychology is a good attitude. I was trained in the four last things.
>
> Most of what I do is private and stays that way.
>
> The way in is the Assess. Half a day, and by the end of it we both know whether the six months
> makes sense.
>
> It should fit who you are now.

**Notes on the draft.** It opens on her thesis, not on her. It uses the category refusal as the
positioning rather than as a disclaimer. Every credential is from the confirmed list in doc 07, and
the private bank stays unnamed. It states the offer once, plainly, near the end. It lands on one of
her own lines, under twelve words.

**Watch:** the third paragraph is three parallel clauses. It survives the list-of-three ban only
because it is a refusal sequence rather than a list of qualities, and because the fourth clause
breaks the pattern. **Flag it for her.** If she reads it as a list of three, cut the therapist
clause.

## Photograph

One change only: it should look like a woman who has been in the room and has nothing left to
prove. Not a laughing stock portrait, not arms folded, no stage shot with a headset, no blurred
conference background.

---

## Experience and the rest

Kept factual and short. No responsibilities lists, no achievements, no "passionate about". The
private bank stays unnamed here too, which means the entry reads as the role and the period, not
the employer.

**Skills, recommendations, endorsements:** leave them, do not cultivate them. Chasing endorsements
at this altitude reads exactly as the thing the whole position refuses.

---

## The one metric this document is judged on

**Profile visit to application rate.** It is the single unmeasured stage in doc 13 with the most
leverage, and it is entirely inside our control. Baseline it in week one, before the rewrite goes
live, so the rewrite can be measured against something.
