# 16 · DM and Inbound Playbook

**Status:** DRAFT for the shapes. The governing rule is SETTLED and absolute.

> **No DM goes out under her name before her approval.**
> Decided 15 September. Not negotiable, and not covered by any standing approval that may later be
> granted for comments.

Every DM is **red lane**. It stops, it goes to her directly, named as red, before anything is even
written.

---

## What this document is for

Not outreach. There is no cold outreach in this system and there will not be. This covers what
happens when somebody comes to her: a reply to a post, a substantive comment, a connection
request, a message.

---

## The inbound ladder

```
  Comment on her post
        |
        v
  Reply, on the post, inside the 30 minute window     <- doc 18, green once O2 lands
        |
        v
  If a real conversation starts, it stays on the post
        |
        v
  Only if they message first does anything move to DM  <- red lane, always
        |
        v
  The answer, and the profile does the rest
```

**Nothing is ever moved to DM by us.** If the conversation deserves privacy, they will move it.
The profile carries the Assess. A comment earns the profile visit or it earns nothing.

---

## When somebody messages her

Four situations. Each one has a shape, and each one still goes to her before it is sent.

### 1 · A direct question about working together

Short. States what exists. Does not sell, does not qualify by message, does not book by message.

**Draft shape**
> The way in is the Assess. Half a day, and by the end of it we both know whether the six months
> makes sense. The application is in my Featured section.

Under forty words. No enthusiasm, no "I'd love to", no calendar link pasted into the thread. The
application exists precisely so this conversation does not have to happen in a DM.

### 2 · A substantive reply to something she wrote

Treat it as a comment that happens to be private. Same craft as doc 04, same rations, same
landings. No offer, ever, in this situation. **This is the one most likely to be mishandled**,
because it feels like an opening and it is not.

### 3 · A request to pick her brain, jump on a quick call, or partner

Declined plainly, once, without a counter-offer.

> Thank you for asking. I keep that time for client work.

No referral, no "but here is a resource", no softening. The refusal is the position.

### 4 · A pitch, a solicitation, or an agency

No reply. Not a polite one. Her attention is the scarce asset and a reply is a reward.

## What never appears in a message under her name

- A link to anything, other than the Featured section named in words
- A calendar link
- A price, unless she puts it there herself
- "Happy to discuss", "my door is open", "let me know if you'd like to chat"
- Any question that functions as qualification
- Voice notes
- Anything longer than sixty words
- Emoji, exclamation marks, em dashes. Doc 04 applies in full

---

## Connection requests

**Incoming.** Accepted if the person is plausibly in tier one or tier two of doc 18, or is simply
a real person in the territory. No note required, no reply required.

**Outgoing.** Rare, and only to somebody she has actually met or worked with. No note that
mentions what she does. If a note is sent at all, it names where they met and stops.

---

## The comment to conversation to application path, as it actually works

This is worth stating plainly, because the temptation to shortcut it will be constant.

1. She comments on the right person's post, adding the part it left out
2. That person visits her profile
3. The profile answers *is she actually who that implied*
4. The Featured section carries the Assess
5. They apply, or they do not

**There is no step where we message them.** The entire mechanism is that she does not need to.
That restraint is the product demonstrating itself, which is why breaking it costs more than the
one client it might win.

---

## If she ever asks for outreach

It is her account and her decision. Two things have to be true first, and they should be said out
loud.

1. The profile rewrite in doc 14 is live and signed off
2. The application to Assess numbers exist, so we know whether the problem is actually reach

Most requests for outreach are a response to a slow month. Doc 13 has the order of investigation,
and outreach is not on it.
