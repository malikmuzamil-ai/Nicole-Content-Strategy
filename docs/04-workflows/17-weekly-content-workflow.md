# 17 · Weekly Content Workflow

**Status:** SETTLED.
**The constraint that shapes everything:** Nicole gives roughly seventy minutes a week. Forty for
the brain dump, thirty for approvals. **If her side ever exceeds ninety minutes, the system is
failing and it is the desk's fault, not hers.**

---

## The week

| Day | Time | Who | What | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 09:00 | Publish | Post 1 goes live. 30/30 window runs | Post live, window logged |
| **Mon** | 16:00 | Nicole + Desk | **Brain dump call, 30 to 40 min** | Recording, transcript |
| **Tue** | 09:00 | Publish | Post 2 goes live. 30/30 window runs | Post live, window logged |
| **Tue** | pm | Desk | Three post briefs against the calendar and the bank | 3 briefs |
| **Wed** | am | Desk | Drafting | 3 drafts |
| **Wed** | pm | Desk | **Quality gate, fourteen points.** Failures are rewrites | Gated drafts |
| **Wed** | pm | Design | Cards, carousel or cheatsheet, from written brief only | Assets |
| **Thu** | 09:00 | Publish | Post 3 goes live, the saveable one. 30/30 window runs | Post live, window logged |
| **Thu** | 12:00 | Desk | **Batch to Nicole:** next week's three drafts plus held comments | One message, one batch |
| **Thu** | by 18:00 | Nicole | Approve, cut, or send back | Approvals |
| **Fri** | am | Desk | Rework anything sent back. Schedule next week | Scheduled |
| **Fri** | 15:00 | Desk | **Weekly report** | Report to Nicole |

**Publishing days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday.** Three a week is the ceiling.

## The brain dump

Forty minutes, recorded, unstructured on her side and structured on ours. **She talks. We do not
interview her into our own frame.**

The questions are in `docs/05-templates/brain-dump-prompt.md`. Five or six, sent the Friday before
so she has seen them, never worked through mechanically on the call.

**What we are actually listening for**
- A sentence she says twice in different words. That is a post
- A physical object. **Write it down verbatim.** These are the scarcest asset we have, see O5
- A moment she starts to tell and then stops. Ask once, then leave it
- Any sentence that begins "the thing nobody says is"
- Her irritation. It is the most reliable signal of a real position

**What we do not do.** Ask her to approve topics on the call. She approves drafts, not ideas.
Approving an idea makes the draft harder for her to refuse honestly later.

---

## Briefing

Every post gets a brief. No exceptions, including a six word card.
Template: `docs/05-templates/post-brief-template.md`.

A brief that cannot answer these four is not a brief.

1. **Pillar.** Exactly one
2. **The line.** The actual words, not a description of them
3. **Source.** Traced. No source, no post. Doc 07
4. **What the reader does in the three seconds after.** Doc 08

---

## Drafting

Write in her register, doc 04. Read aloud before it leaves the desk. Three drafts, roughly two and
a half hours.

**Draft the caption last.** The card or the first line is the post. A caption written first always
ends up explaining the card, which kills it.

---

## The batch to Nicole

**One message a week.** Not a drip of individual asks. Her attention is the scarce resource and
fragmenting it is the fastest way to lose the rhythm.

The batch contains, in this order:

1. **Three drafts** for next week, each with pillar, format, and the source in one line
2. **Held comments**, amber lane, with the target post in one line each
3. **Anything red**, named as red, at the top if it is urgent
4. **What we need from her**, if anything. Maximum two asks

Nothing else. No performance review, no engagement stats, no explanation of the craft unless she
asks.

**Her response takes one of three forms** and all three are complete answers: approve, cut, or one
word back. "Softer", "no", "not that one". A one word cut is not a request for discussion.

---

## Scheduling and publishing

Scheduled Friday for the week ahead. Published at 09:00 CET, which is the start of the window in
doc 18.

**A post is never published without the window.** If nobody can run the 30/30 around it, the post
moves to the next slot. A post that lands with no engagement pass is a post that underperforms and
then teaches us the wrong thing about the idea.

---

## When a week goes wrong

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| No brain dump that week | Pull from the bank, doc 11. Thirty six ideas is three months of runway |
| Nicole does not approve in time | **The slot is missed.** Nothing publishes. A missed slot costs one day |
| A draft fails the gate twice | Kill it. Do not rewrite a third time, the idea is wrong, not the words |
| Design not ready for Thursday | Thursday's saveable post moves to the following Tuesday. It never becomes an image post to make the date |
| A week has to lose a post | It loses Monday. **Never Thursday** |

**Two missed slots and no breaches is a good week.** One breach and twelve perfect posts is not.

---

## The standing rules on cadence

1. Three a week is a ceiling, not a floor to defend by cutting corners. Watchpoint 01
2. Make Me Think, twice a month at most. Watchpoint 02
3. Never respond to a slow month by posting more often. Doc 13
4. No weeks skipped. Consistency is one of the four bars in doc 20
