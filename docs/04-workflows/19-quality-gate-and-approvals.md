# 19 · Quality Gate and Approvals

**Status:** SETTLED.
**Run on:** every post, every carousel, every cheatsheet, every comment, every DM, every profile
line, every proposal. Nothing is exempt for being short.

**Any failure is a rewrite, not an edit.** A draft that is patched to pass a gate point usually
fails a different one, because the fault was in the thinking.

---

## The fourteen points

### Positioning, 1 to 3

1. **Does it trace back to the thesis?** They have achieved everything. They have not achieved
   themselves. If the line to that sentence takes more than one step, it is somebody else's post
2. **Which pillar, and only one?** A post that fits two has not decided what it does to the reader
3. **Where is the permission?** If there is none, why does the post still earn its place

### Voice, 4 to 8

4. **Read it aloud.** Can you hear her saying it across a table. This point alone catches more than
   the other thirteen
5. **Any banned word?** Doc 04 section 6. Including **coach**, **midlife**, **retirement**, and the
   whole softness set
6. **Any banned formatting?** Em dash, en dash, emoji, exclamation mark, hashtag, bullet character,
   trailing ellipsis, link in the body
7. **Any list of three?** Three adjectives, three parallel clauses, three bullets opening the same
   way. **The clearest machine tell in the material**
8. **The rations.** More than one negative definition, witness line, object, abstraction with
   manners, wit beat, hedge or signature line

### The reader, 9 to 11

9. **Does it name a state at somebody?** Empty, numb, caged, stuck, isolated, lonely, burnt out,
   exhausted. **Test: would a board member reading this embarrass the person it describes**
10. **Does it flatter?** Delete the flattery and see whether anything is left
11. **Does it praise vulnerability?** Brave, courageous, "thank you for being so open". She advises
    high performers against displaying vulnerability at work

### The charter, 12 and 13

12. **Does every proper noun, number, date and quote trace?** Doc 07. No source, no post
13. **Any client detail identifiable in combination?** Not "did we name them". **Could somebody who
    knows this person read it and know who it is**

### The landing, 14

14. **Is the last line the shortest, under twelve words, turned back to the reader, and
    unresolved?** No question to the reader, no offer, no link, no thank you, no moral, no summary

---

## The two that stop a draft dead

Points **12 and 13** are not quality failures. They are breaches. A draft that fails either one
does not get rewritten in the same sitting and does not get published that week. It goes to her,
named as red.

Everything else is a rewrite.

---

## The lanes

| Lane | What it covers | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | A type and a target she has already approved, using only cleared material | Goes out. Logged after |
| **Amber** | A new type, a new kind of target, a first use of one of her lines, or anything the desk is unsure about | Held. Goes to her in the Thursday batch. Never placed live |
| **Red** | Any DM, anything touching a client, anything about a named person, anything on a death or illness, anything that could read as a stance | **Stops.** Goes to her directly, named as red, before anything is written |

**Green lane requires her standing approval and she has not given it yet. Until she does,
everything is amber.** Open decision O2.

**No DM goes out under her name before her approval.** Decided 15 September. Not covered by any
standing approval.

## The approval loop

```
  Draft
    |
    v
  Fourteen point gate            <- desk. Failures are rewrites
    |
    v
  Read aloud                     <- desk. Non-negotiable
    |
    v
  Lane assigned                  <- desk
    |
    +--- red ---> straight to Nicole, named as red, nothing published in that slot
    |
    v
  Thursday batch                 <- one message, everything in it
    |
    v
  Nicole: approve / cut / one word back
    |
    v
  Publish, or rework once
```

**A draft that fails the gate twice is killed, not rewritten a third time.** The idea is wrong, not
the words.

---

## What a "one word back" means

She responds in one of three ways and all three are complete answers.

| Her response | What it means | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Approved | Publish as written | Publish. Do not improve it afterwards |
| Cut | The idea is wrong | Retire it in doc 11, with the reason |
| One word: "softer", "no", "not that one" | Exactly what it says | Rework once, or drop it. **Not a request for discussion** |

Never ask her to explain a cut. The explanation costs more of her seventy minutes than the post
was worth.

---

## The hard case

A post, comment or message touching a death, an illness or a loss.

**Default until she rules:** under fifteen words. No frame, no wisdom, no equation, no object, no
turn. Her craft is switched off. Sounding like a person matters more than sounding like Nicole.

**Flag it for her ruling rather than deciding it silently.** Open decision O3.

---

## When in doubt

Do not resolve it quietly and do not resolve it under time pressure.

1. Mark it **red** in the desk log
2. Write one line naming exactly what is uncertain
3. Send it to her directly, outside the batch
4. **Publish nothing in that slot**

A missed slot costs one day. A breach costs the position.
