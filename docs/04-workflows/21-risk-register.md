# 21 · Risk Register

**Status:** SETTLED. Reviewed on the first Monday of each month.
**Written the way we would want it written for ourselves.** Nothing here is fatal. All of it is
cheaper to fix now than in month four.

---

## 01 · Too much volume, not enough substance

**The risk.** Three posts a week becomes a quota to hit rather than a ceiling to respect, and the
account fills with posts that exist because Thursday exists.

> "We can't just be a content machine."
> Nicole

**Early signal.** A post goes into the calendar before it has a source. A brief that cannot answer
"what does the reader do in the three seconds after".
**The fix.** Publish two that week. A missed slot costs one day.
**Owner.** Desk.

---

## 02 · The paradox line stops landing

**The risk.** Make Me Think works because it is rare. Run weekly instead of twice a month, it
stops feeling like a punch in the belly and becomes a tic.

**Early signal.** Two Make Me Think posts in one month already scheduled and a third suggested.
Reaction volume high, comment depth low.
**The fix.** Hard cap at two per month. It is already in doc 08 and it is the cap most likely to be
argued with, because those posts perform loudly.
**Owner.** Desk, enforced at the calendar, not at the draft.

---

## 03 · A credential or client detail slips in early

**The risk.** The highest cost failure in the register, and the most likely to happen on a good
day rather than a bad one, because it happens when a post would obviously be stronger with it.

**Early signal.** A draft where a detail makes the story land and nobody can name where it came
from. A sentence that begins "she once worked with".
**The fix.** Doc 07. Every unconfirmed detail, and every client detail specific enough to identify
somebody, stays out until Nicole clears it herself directly. **Even on a day a post would clearly
be stronger with it.**
**Owner.** Nicole holds the only key. The desk holds the discipline.

---

## 04 · Somebody else claims this ground first

**The risk.** Modern Elder Academy has a real audience and a proven playbook. The ground she is
standing on is unusually well defined and currently unoccupied at this altitude.

**Early signal.** Her exact language appearing in somebody else's feed. Not a crisis, but worth
knowing.
**The fix.** **Showing up consistently now is what keeps this ground.** There is no clever answer
to this one. Consistency is the answer.
**Owner.** Everybody. It is what consistency is actually for.
**Note.** Named competitors are never mentioned publicly. Doc 07 rule seven.

---

## 05 · Staying classy against needing reach

**The risk.** Not loud, not cheap, set against the volume of readers needed to reach twelve Assess
sessions. Depth over reach is the right call at this price. **It should stay a choice, not an
accident.**

**Early signal.** A month where the conversation turns to how to get more eyes rather than which
eyes.
**The fix.** Doc 13, the diagnostic order. Reach is the fifth thing to question, not the first. If
reach genuinely is the constraint, the honest answer is more comments on the right posts, not
louder posts.
**Owner.** Reviewed monthly, doc 20.

---

## 06 · Advisor, coach, and the line between

**The risk.** The word slips into a caption, a reply, a bio or a conversation, and the category
refusal that the whole position rests on goes with it.

**Settled:** advisor is how she is described **publicly**, not a claim about who she is privately.
**Early signal.** Anyone on the team using "coaching" as a convenient verb in an internal document.
It migrates outward from there.
**The fix.** **The word coach never appears publicly.** It is on the banned list in doc 04 and it
is checked at gate point 5.
**Owner.** Desk, at the gate.

---

## 07 · Content gets ahead of the offer

**The risk.** A post starts hinting at cohorts, a programme, or group work before that side of the
business is ready, and it describes a business that does not exist yet.

**Early signal.** The word "we" appearing where "I" belongs. Any sentence implying scale. Anything
about the Academy.
**The fix.** Nothing downstream is referenced publicly until decision O6 is settled. Doc 06.
**Owner.** Nicole settles O6. Until she does, the answer is no.

---

## The one that matters most

**There is no content plan here without Nicole in the room after it.** Every application still has
to be met by one person, showing up.

The job of content is to bring in **the right four clients, not the most applicants.** A month that
produces forty applications from people who want a coach has done more damage than a month that
produced none, because it spends her time and it teaches us the wrong thing about what is working.

---

## Review

First Monday of each month, alongside doc 20. For each of the seven: has the early signal
appeared, has the owner changed, is the fix still the right fix.

A risk that has not moved in three months is not necessarily safe. It may just be unobserved.
Check the signal, not the memory.
