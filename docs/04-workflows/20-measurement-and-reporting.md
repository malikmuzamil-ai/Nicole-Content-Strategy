## The four bars

Diagnostics, not targets. Missing one for two consecutive weeks triggers a review, not a panic.

| Signal | Bar | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Save rate** | Saves at least half of likes on Carousel and Cheatsheet posts | Saves are the only engagement signal that correlates with the reader treating it as useful |
| **Comment depth** | 8 or more real comments per Teach Me post | The earliest honest signal that the right people are reading. A real comment is one that adds something, not "great post" |
| **Consistency** | 3 times a week, no weeks skipped | Most funnel problems are attendance problems |
| **Assess applications** | Tracked weekly from day one | **The only number that actually matters** |

---

## What we deliberately do not report

Not because they are unmeasurable, but because reporting them changes behaviour in the wrong
direction.

- Follower count
- Impressions, as a headline number
- Likes, in isolation
- Any comparison against another account
- Engagement rate as a single blended figure
- Anything that would make "post more often" look like the answer

Impressions are recorded, because they are one half of the profile visit rate. They are never
reported as an achievement.

## The weekly report

Sent Friday 15:00. Template: `docs/05-templates/weekly-report-template.md`.
**One page. Applications first.**

```
1 · APPLICATIONS          this week, running total, and which post each named
2 · THE FOUR BARS         cleared or missed, one line each
3 · PUBLISHED             three posts, pillar, format, saves / comments / impressions
4 · THE WINDOW            comments placed, comments refused, replies given
5 · NEXT WEEK             three drafts, awaiting her approval
6 · OPEN                  anything needed from her. Maximum two asks
```

Nothing else. No commentary on the craft, no celebration of a good post, no apology for a quiet
one.

## Reading the numbers honestly

Three traps, all of which will come up.

**A high performing Make Me Think post is not evidence.** That pillar is built to provoke and it
generates reaction by design. Applications come disproportionately from Teach Me and Understand
Me. Judging the account on its loudest post is how the calendar drifts toward provocation and the
funnel quietly empties.

**A quiet month with two applications beat a loud month with none.** Say so plainly in the report
rather than leading with the reach figure.

**Thirty six posts is a small sample.** Almost nothing in the first ninety days is statistically
meaningful. Patterns are worth noting. Conclusions are worth resisting until there are three
months of them.

## The diagnostic order, when applications are slow

From doc 13, repeated here because this is where it will be needed.

1. Consistency. Three a week, no weeks skipped
2. The profile. Doc 14. Has the rewrite gone live
3. Pillar balance. Rebalance toward Teach Me and Understand Me
4. Comment depth on Teach Me posts
5. Only then, the offer or the price

**Never** respond to a slow month by posting more often.

## What success looks like at day 90

Not four clients, necessarily. Four clients is the target and targets are missed for reasons that
have nothing to do with the work.

What has to be true at day 90, regardless:

- Thirty six posts published, no week skipped
- Zero charter breaches
- The two unmeasured funnel stages in doc 13 now measured
- The Assess to client ratio replaced with a real number, however small the sample
- The idea bank still above twenty
- Her time still inside ninety minutes a week

If all six are true and there are two clients rather than four, the system works and the maths
needs adjusting. If none are true and there are four clients, something else brought them in and
we should find out what.
