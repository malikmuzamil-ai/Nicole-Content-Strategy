# 09 · Format Playbook

**Status:** DRAFT. The format mix per pillar is settled. The specs are ours and need one pass from
her before month two.
**Purpose:** so that "make it a carousel" means the same thing to every person who touches it.

---

## Format by pillar

| Pillar | Permitted formats | Not permitted |
|---|---|---|
| Make Me Think | Text post, image post | Carousel. A provocation with eight slides is no longer a provocation |
| Understand Me | Text post, cheatsheet | Video, until much later |
| Teach Me | Carousel, cheatsheet, text post | Image post. The pillar exists to be reopened |
| Entertain Me | Text post, image post, video from month three | Carousel, cheatsheet |

**First thirty days override.** All twelve posts are image led. No text posts anywhere in month
one. See doc 12.

---

## 1 · The image post

**What it is.** One line on a card. The caption carries almost nothing.

| Spec | |
|---|---|
| Dimensions | 1080 x 1350 (4:5) preferred, 1080 x 1080 acceptable |
| Words on the card | 6 to 25. Past 25 it is a cheatsheet wearing the wrong clothes |
| Caption | 0 to 40 words. Often zero |
| Hook | The card is the hook. The first caption line is not doing that job |
| Source link | Her own first comment, if there is a source |

**When the caption is zero words.** Make Me Think, almost always. The card says it, and anything
underneath explains the joke.

**When the caption earns 30 to 40 words.** Understand Me and Entertain Me, where the card is the
scene and the caption is the quarter turn.

---

## 2 · The carousel

**What it is.** The Teach Me workhorse. Native LinkedIn documents average roughly 7.0 percent
engagement, the highest of any format on the platform, and they are built to be saved.

| Spec | |
|---|---|
| Slides | 8 to 10. Under 8 is thin, over 10 loses the last third of readers |
| Dimensions | 1080 x 1350 per page, exported as PDF |
| Words per slide | 15 to 45. Slide 1 is 6 to 12 |
| Structure | Hook, turn, five to seven idea slides, landing, optional identification |
| Caption | 60 to 120 words. Sets up the document, never summarises it |

**Slide grammar is in doc 05.** No slide numbers as decoration, no progress bars, no swipe prompts.

**The rule that makes carousels work here.** One idea per slide, and the slides are in an order
that could not be shuffled. If any two slides could swap places, it is a list, not an argument.

---

## 3 · The cheatsheet

**What it is.** A single card, built entirely to be saved and reopened. The most valuable asset
per hour of work in the whole plan.

| Spec | |
|---|---|
| Dimensions | 1080 x 1350 |
| Rows | 5 or 6. Never 7, never 4 |
| Words per row | 4 to 14 |
| Title | A scene or a question. Never a promise, never a number |
| Caption | 40 to 90 words, carrying the story the card came from |

**Title test.** "The question that made him go quiet" is a title. "5 signs you have outgrown your
business" is not, and it belongs to the category she is leaving.

**Never** print an instruction to save it on the card.

---

## 4 · The text post

**What it is.** Her natural register, unmediated. From month two onward this becomes the backbone.

| Spec | |
|---|---|
| Length | 90 to 220 words |
| First two lines | Carry the hook. LinkedIn truncates after roughly 210 characters on mobile |
| Line breaks | One idea per line, blank line between |
| Paragraph length | 1 to 2 lines. Never 4 |
| Links | None in the body, ever |
| Landing | Under twelve words, shorter than the line before it |

**The shape that works most often**

```
Line 1     The hook. A statement, not a question.
Line 2     The turn, or the fence.

Lines 3-6  The body. One idea per line.
           One object, or one abstraction with manners. Not both.

Last line  The landing. Short. Unresolved.
```

---

## 5 · Video

**Not before month three.** Tested inside Entertain Me only. Open decision O8: whether she wants it
at all, and on camera or voice over.

| Spec, provisional | |
|---|---|
| Length | 45 to 90 seconds |
| Format | Vertical, 1080 x 1920, captions burned in |
| Content | One field note. One pattern. No framework, no call to action |
| Caption | Same rules as a text post, 60 to 100 words |

If the first test does not clearly outperform an Entertain Me image post on saves and comment
depth, video is dropped without ceremony. It is a test, not a commitment.

---

## What never gets made

- A newsletter, until the posting rhythm has run clean for a quarter
- A poll. It is engagement bait with a respectable face
- A "thread" of any kind
- A repost of her own post with a new caption
- Anything with a countdown, a spot count, or a deadline
- A "lessons from" listicle
- A before and after, in any format
