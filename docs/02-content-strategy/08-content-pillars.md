# 08 · Content Pillars

**Status:** SETTLED, 5 August. These are not our pillars. They are Nicole's own words, kept
exactly as she said them, given a weight and a job.

**Every post fits exactly one pillar.** A post that fits two is a post that has not decided what it
does to the reader, which is the only thing that matters here. The pillars are built around the
effect, not the topic underneath.

---

## The weights

| Pillar | Weight | Job in the funnel | Posts per month, at 12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Make Me Think | 25% | Stops the scroll, provokes before she decides what she thinks | 3 |
| Understand Me | 25% | Recognition. She is understood before she is taught | 3 |
| Teach Me | 30% | Saved and reopened. Carries the heaviest share of the method | 4 |
| Entertain Me | 20% | Warmth and proof, without a case study | 2 |

Rebalance monthly, not weekly. A single week that runs heavy on one pillar is not a problem. A
month that does is.

---

## 1 · Make Me Think · 25%

**Positioning.** One sentence, no caption underneath, built to stop the scroll and provoke a
reaction before the reader has decided what she thinks.

**Intent mix.** Authority 35% · Education 25% · Selling 20% · Trust 20%

**Formats.** Text post, image post.

**Cadence.** Twice a month at most. Her own words: *"a punch in the belly."* More often than that
and it stops working. This is watchpoint 02 in doc 21.

### Her own examples

- The house I love belongs to my ex-wife now.
- The kids I never wanted are the best thing that ever happened to me.
- Everyone worth knowing knows nothing about me.
- I have fans around the world, and I am lonely.

Note the shape. First person, a contradiction held inside one sentence, no resolution, no caption.

### Sub-theme topics

| Topic | The angle underneath it |
|---|---|
| They have achieved everything. They have not achieved themselves | The single sentence that is the entire thesis, said plainly |
| He won the oldest trophy in professional sport, and never got the one verdict he actually wanted | A true story about a famous, driven man, and the question every reader is quietly asking too |
| Most people play not to lose, rather than playing to win | The difference between defending a life and building one |
| A vision anyone can produce in a workshop | Almost nobody survives the first real pressure test after |
| Positive psychology is a good attitude. She was trained in the four last things | Separates her from the entire wellbeing category in one breath |
| Still, quietly, the tiny person being scolded by a parent who has been gone for twenty years | What actually keeps performing long after the audience left |
| We are not dead yet | Against an entire industry that treats the second half of life as a wind down |

## 2 · Understand Me · 25%

**Positioning.** Names what the reader feels before she has the words for it. Not making her
understand something. **Showing her she is understood first.**

**Intent mix.** Trust 40% · Selling 25% · Education 20% · Authority 15%

**Formats.** Text post, cheatsheet.

**The trap in this pillar.** It is one sentence away from naming a state at a reader. Everything in
this pillar is written in the third person or the abstract. See doc 04 section 6.

### Sub-theme topics

| Topic | The angle underneath it |
|---|---|
| Extreme responsibility. A high threshold for pain. Total loyalty | Every one a virtue. Together, they build the trap |
| Nobody imprisons them | Their own best qualities do it, one reliable decision at a time |
| Surrounded by people, and lonely anyway | The gap almost nobody names until she says it out loud herself |
| Nothing is broken | That is exactly what makes it impossible to explain to anyone who could fix a broken thing |
| Success and fulfilment are not the same purchase | Four words that look like synonyms and are not |
| No complaint and no inspiration | The specific flatness that never gets taken seriously because it does not look like a crisis |
| People need permission for that | Named directly, as the thing everyone in this position is quietly waiting for |

## 3 · Teach Me · 30%

**Positioning.** Leaves the reader smarter and able to use it this week. The pillar built to be
saved and reopened, so it carries the heaviest share of the method itself.

**Intent mix.** Education 45% · Authority 25% · Selling 20% · Trust 10%

**Formats.** Carousel, cheatsheet, text post.

**The line to hold.** She does not sell frameworks. She is willing to **show the method working on
one real anonymised decision.** The difference is whether the reader leaves with a tool or with an
understanding of how the thinking goes.

### Sub-theme topics

| Topic | The angle underneath it |
|---|---|
| Assess, Attune, Aspire, Align, Ascend | Five acts, not a workshop weekend. Walked through slide by slide on one real, anonymised decision |
| Why a life operating system survives what a vision statement does not | The practical difference, not the poetic one |
| Separate the gravel from the gold | What still genuinely belongs to you, and what you have only ever carried out of habit |
| The 48-hour resonance protocol | Taught once and kept for life. Built so the next decision does not need her in the room |
| A coach, a consultant and a therapist, before she called | Three different rooms, three vocabularies, and the one question that mattered never got asked in any of them |
| The skill that built the company is usually the one now running it into a wall | Almost nobody tells the person still using it, because it is the same skill everyone praises them for |
| Two years from now, could you explain why you decided this | The record almost nobody keeps, and why a decision made under pressure gets re-litigated in hindsight |

## 4 · Entertain Me · 20%

**Positioning.** Her own mechanic: **a detail from somewhere she has actually stood, paired with
the exact strategy a reader is running right now that does not work.**

**Intent mix.** Trust 45% · Education 20% · Selling 20% · Authority 15%

**Formats.** Text post, image post, video from month three.

**The charter applies hardest here.** Every client detail stays anonymised and composite. No name,
company, or identifying combination of facts appears without her direct sign off first. See doc 07.

### Sub-theme topics

| Topic | The angle underneath it |
|---|---|
| We finally have you back | What a spouse notices, months in, before the person going through it fully does. Nobody can accuse a family of marketing |
| A comfortable, mediocre life, and a decision to climb higher anyway | What was waiting underneath the ambition once he stopped climbing toward somebody else's ladder |
| Started with nothing in her twenties, built something enormous, still felt like a fraud on the good days | Told lightly, never as a brag, always as recognition |
| What changes for a family in six months | Almost nothing to do with the business. More time. More care. A relationship that got its person back |
| A moment nothing in twenty five years prepared her for | |
| Field notes from rooms most people only ever see from the outside | The pattern that repeats, no matter which room it is |
| What surprised her most about advising people who already have everything | And then finding out everything was never actually the target |

## Choosing the pillar, when a draft could be two

Ask what the reader does in the three seconds after she finishes reading.

| She does this | The pillar is |
|---|---|
| Stops, and argues with it in her head | Make Me Think |
| Goes quiet, because somebody just described her | Understand Me |
| Saves it, and expects to reopen it | Teach Me |
| Smiles, then does not | Entertain Me |

If the honest answer is "scrolls on", the post does not exist yet.

## What we have given up on purpose

Fewer, better, quieter. Not a limitation. The thing a louder competitor cannot copy without losing
the audience that made them loud in the first place.

- **Bait and engagement pods.** The platform pushes both down anyway, and they would cheapen the
  account even if it did not
- **Writing that sounds AI generated.** Every draft is read aloud in her cadence before it goes up
- **The standard case study format.** Before, after, logo, quote. The category she is leaving
- **Any client detail that could identify somebody,** even indirectly
- **Any credential not already confirmed directly by her**
- **Heavy personal storytelling.** She is reserved about her own stories. Content stays built on
  the questions, not on mining her life for material
