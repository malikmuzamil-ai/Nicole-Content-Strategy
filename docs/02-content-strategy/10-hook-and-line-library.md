# 10 · Hook and Line Library

**Status:** DRAFT. Every line traced to her material is marked `HERS`. Every line built by the desk
in her register is marked `DESK` and needs her red pen before it publishes.
**Use:** never as a fill-in-the-blank. A hook is chosen because it is true of the post underneath
it, not because the slot needs filling.

---

## How to read this

`HERS` verbatim from her published posts, her spoken material, or a dated brief. Safe to use.
`DESK` written in her register, untested. Amber lane. Goes in the batch, never straight out.

## Opening moves, ranked

The six openers from doc 04, with a worked example of each.

| # | Move | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The quarter turn** | Take the noun and give it a different verb. Delegation becomes an act of ego rather than a skill |
| 2 | **The declined obvious** | `HERS` I am less interested in telling CEOs to sleep more. They know |
| 3 | **The fence** | The study is solid and the result is real. It does not prove what it is being used to prove |
| 4 | **The witness** | `HERS` I have spent years around people with considerable power |

**Never open with** credentials, a statistic as a hook, "As someone who", "This reminds me of", a
rhetorical question, or a restatement.

## Make Me Think · the paradox lines

The hardest format she has. One sentence, no caption, no resolution. **Twice a month at most.**

`HERS` The house I love belongs to my ex-wife now.
`HERS` The kids I never wanted are the best thing that ever happened to me.
`HERS` Everyone worth knowing knows nothing about me.
`HERS` I have fans around the world, and I am lonely.
`HERS` They have achieved everything. They have not achieved themselves.
`HERS` We are not dead yet.
`HERS` Most people play not to lose, rather than playing to win.

`DESK` Delegation is an act of ego.
`DESK` Positive psychology is a good attitude. I was trained in the four last things.
`DESK` He won the oldest trophy in professional sport. He never got the verdict he actually wanted.
`DESK` A vision survives the workshop. Almost nothing survives the first pressure test after.

**The shape.** First person where possible. A contradiction held inside one sentence. No caption
explaining it. The reader supplies her own examples, and that supply is the entire mechanism.

## Understand Me · the recognition lines

Third person or abstract. Never aimed at the reader. See the state word ban in doc 04.

`HERS` People need permission for that. They have no access to their own abundance.
`HERS` Nothing is broken. That is exactly what makes it impossible to explain.
`HERS` Surrounded by people, and lonely anyway.

`DESK` Extreme responsibility. A high threshold for pain. Total loyalty. Every one of them a virtue.
`DESK` Nobody imprisons them. Their own best qualities do it, one reliable decision at a time.
`DESK` No complaint and no inspiration. It never gets taken seriously because it does not look like a crisis.
`DESK` Rich, but unknown. Or worse: unknown, and unnecessary.
`DESK` The calendars that were packed now lie eerily blank. And so does the sense of who you are.
`DESK` You believe a different future is available to other people. Just not to you.

Note: the last two are permitted in the second person because they describe a **belief** and a
**circumstance**, not an inner state. That is the line. "You feel empty" is banned. "You believe a
different future is available to other people" is recognition.

## Teach Me · the useful openers

A number earns its place here and nowhere else, and only when it is genuinely citable.

`DESK` Executives who delegate effectively generate roughly a third more revenue than those who cannot let go.
`DESK` Two years from now, could you actually explain why you decided this?

## Abstractions with manners

`HERS` Questions without an alarm bell wait politely in the corridor.
`HERS` One rather inconvenient question sits quietly at the table.
`HERS` The urgent has a nasty habit of impersonating the important.
`HERS` The higher you rise, the more truth arrives pre-edited.

---

## Hooks that are banned outright

Anything beginning: Great post · So true · Unpopular opinion · Let that sink in · Here is the
thing · I'll say it louder · Nobody talks about this · Most people get this wrong ·
The truth is · Read that again · Save this post · Agree?

Anything ending in a question asked of the reader. Anything containing a number promise
("7 ways", "3 signs"). Anything that opens on her credentials.

---

## Before a line leaves this document

1. Is it `HERS`, or is it `DESK` and therefore amber
2. Is it true of the post underneath it, or is it decoration
3. Has it been used in the last four weeks
4. Read aloud: can you hear her saying it across a table
