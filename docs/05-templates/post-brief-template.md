# Template · Post Brief

Every post. No exceptions, including a six word card. Copy this block, fill it, do not skip a
field. A field you cannot fill is the post telling you something.

---

```
POST ID         MT-00 / UM-00 / TM-00 / EM-00
DATE            Mon / Tue / Thu, DD Month
PILLAR          exactly one
FORMAT          image / text / carousel / cheatsheet / video
INTENT          education / trust / authority / selling

THE LINE
                The actual words. Not a description of them.


CAPTION
                The actual words, or "none".


ANGLE
                Two or three sentences. Why this line and not a nearby one.


CONTEXT
                What it does to the reader, and why it lands with her specifically.


SOURCE          Traced. Her material with a date, or a named public source.
                No source, no post.

SOURCE LINK     Goes in her own first comment. Never in the body. Or "none".

THREE SECONDS   What does the reader do in the three seconds after.
                Argues / goes quiet / saves it / smiles then does not.

PERMISSION      Where is the permission in this post. If none, why it still earns its place.

LANE            green / amber / red

GATE            14/14 passed, read aloud, date and initials
```

---

## The four that make it a brief

If these four are not answered, it is a note, not a brief.

1. **Pillar.** Exactly one
2. **The line.** The actual words
3. **Source.** Traced
4. **Three seconds.** What the reader does

---

## Worked example

```
POST ID         TM-08
DATE            Thu, 16 October
PILLAR          Teach Me
FORMAT          Cheatsheet
INTENT          Education

THE LINE        The question that made him go quiet.

CAPTION         He listed everything he had. The business. The money. The marriage.
                The health.

                I asked what he had of himself.

                He did not answer for a long time.

ANGLE           Open on the scene, not the framework. The pause is the post. A listicle
                of the same five rows would be half the asset.

CONTEXT         Five rows, one honest answer each. Success against fulfilment.
                Performance against feeling alive. A role against real identity. Hustle
                and safety against calling. People around them against anyone who
                actually knows them. The single most saveable asset in the plan.

SOURCE          Nicole's own five gap structure, 11 August positioning brief,
                reframed narratively.

SOURCE LINK     none

THREE SECONDS   Saves it, and expects to reopen it.

PERMISSION      The last row. Naming that nobody actually knows her makes it allowed
                to want somebody to.

LANE            amber, first use of the five gap table publicly

GATE            14/14, read aloud 14 Oct, PP
```

Note the caption: four short lines, a blank line before the turn, and it stops. It does not
explain the card and it does not ask the reader anything.
