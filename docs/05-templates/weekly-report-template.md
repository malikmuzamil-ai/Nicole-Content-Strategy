# Template · Weekly Report

Sent Friday 15:00. **One page. Applications first.** Nothing else.

No commentary on the craft, no celebration of a good post, no apology for a quiet one. If
something needs discussing, it goes in section 6 as an ask.

## Rules for writing it

- **Applications first, always.** Even in a week with none. Especially in a week with none
- A quiet month with two applications beat a loud month with none. **Say so plainly** rather than
  leading with reach
- Impressions are recorded, never presented as an achievement
- Real comments are counted by hand. The automated number includes "great post"
- Refusals are reported as work done, not as a shortfall
- Never propose posting more often as a response to a slow week
- Two asks maximum. Her seventy minutes are the constraint the whole system is built around
