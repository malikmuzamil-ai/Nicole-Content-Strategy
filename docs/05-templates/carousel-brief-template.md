## The two tests before it goes to design

1. **Could any two slides swap places?** If yes, it is a list, not an argument. Reorder or cut
2. **Does slide 8 resolve anything?** It should not. She undercuts the crescendo rather than
   building one

---

## Design will not do these, so do not ask

- Slide numbers as decoration
- A progress bar
- A "swipe left" arrow. The platform supplies that affordance
- More than one accent colour on a card
- More than one dark card in a set
- A gradient, a glow, a soft shadow
- Three icons in a row
- A logo lockup on a statement slide
