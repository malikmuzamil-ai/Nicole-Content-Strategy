# Template · Cheatsheet Brief

The most valuable asset per hour of work in the plan. Built entirely to be saved and reopened.

Specs in doc 09. Visual grammar in doc 05.

---

```
CHEATSHEET ID   UM-00 / TM-00
DATE            Thu, DD Month
PILLAR          Understand Me or Teach Me
ROWS            5 or 6. Never 7, never 4.
SOURCE          Traced
LANE            green / amber / red
```

---

## The card

```
TITLE           A scene or a question. Never a promise, never a number.

COLUMN A        Label, kicker style, uppercase, terracotta
COLUMN B        Label, kicker style, uppercase, terracotta

ROW 1           A                          |  B
ROW 2           A                          |  B
ROW 3           A                          |  B
ROW 4           A                          |  B
ROW 5           A                          |  B
ROW 6           A                          |  B          (optional)

FOOTER          nicolebrandes.com, 10.5px, --ink2. Nothing else.
```

Four to fourteen words per cell. Hairline rules between rows, never a filled table.

---

## Caption

```
CAPTION         40 to 90 words. Carries the story the card came from.
                The card is the reference. The caption is the reason it exists.
```

---

## Title test

| Is a title | Is not |
|---|---|
| The question that made him go quiet | 5 signs you have outgrown your business |
| What a spouse notices first | The ultimate founder checklist |
| Two years from now, could you explain why you decided this | 7 succession mistakes to avoid |

A number in the title puts the card in the category she is leaving.

---

## Never on the card

- An instruction to save it
- An instruction to share it
- A call to action of any kind
- Her photograph
- A logo
- More than one accent colour
