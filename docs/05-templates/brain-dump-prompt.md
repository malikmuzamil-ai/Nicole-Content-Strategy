# Template · Brain Dump Prompt

Sent to Nicole the Friday before the Monday call, so she has seen the questions. **Never worked
through mechanically on the call.** She talks. We do not interview her into our own frame.

Thirty to forty minutes. Recorded. Five or six questions, of which we usually get through three.

---

## The standing five

These rotate but the shape holds. Each one is built to produce a sentence rather than a topic.

1. **What did somebody say to you this week that you have not stopped thinking about?**
2. **What is the thing nobody in your world says out loud, that everybody knows?**
3. **What irritated you this week?** Professionally. Her irritation is the most reliable signal of
   a real position
4. **Describe a room.** Any room she has actually been in, this week or twenty years ago. Where it
   was, what was on the table, who was not saying something
5. **What did a client work out for herself that you had been waiting for her to work out?**

---

## The rotating sixth

One of these, chosen against the pillar that is thinnest in the bank.

**For Make Me Think**
- What is true about your life that sounds like a contradiction when you say it plainly?
- What does everybody in your category believe that you think is simply wrong?

**For Understand Me**
- What does somebody in this position feel that they would never find the words for?
- What do people get wrong about what it is actually like at that altitude?

**For Teach Me**
- Walk me through one decision, start to finish, with the name taken out
- What is the smallest thing you teach somebody that they keep for the rest of their life?

**For Entertain Me**
- **Give me an object.** Something physical, from somewhere you have actually stood
- What surprised you most about advising people who already have everything?

---

## What we are listening for

| Signal | What to do |
|---|---|
| A sentence she says twice in different words | That is a post. Write down both versions |
| **A physical object** | **Write it down verbatim.** The scarcest asset we have. Open decision O5 |
| A moment she starts to tell and then stops | Ask once. Then leave it |
| "The thing nobody says is..." | Stop and get the whole sentence |
| Her irritation | Follow it. It is a position, not a mood |
| A number, a date, a name | Confirm it on the call. It becomes a source or it does not exist |

---

## What we do not do on the call

- **Ask her to approve topics.** She approves drafts, not ideas. Approving an idea makes the draft
  harder for her to refuse honestly later
- Read her last week's numbers
- Ask her to write anything
- Explain the craft
- Run the questions in order, if the conversation has gone somewhere better
- Ask for a client story. If one comes, it comes. Then it goes through doc 07

---

## After the call

Same day, while the recording is fresh.

1. Transcribe
2. Pull every candidate line verbatim. **Verbatim, not paraphrased.** A paraphrase is already a
   loss of voice
3. New objects go to doc 10, marked `HERS`
4. New ideas go to doc 11, with the call date as the source
5. Anything she confirmed as a credential goes to doc 02, the decision log
6. Anything she ruled on goes to doc 02 as well

---

## The standing ask, every week

Two open decisions get raised on every call until they are closed.

- **O2.** Standing green lane approval for engagement comments
- **O5.** Three or four more physical objects

Both are cheap for her and both unblock real work. Raising them takes ninety seconds.
