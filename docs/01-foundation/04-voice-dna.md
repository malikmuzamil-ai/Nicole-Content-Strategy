# 04 · Voice DNA

**Status:** SETTLED. Derived from her published posts, her spoken material and the Comment Desk
standard.
**Applies to:** every public asset. Posts, carousels, cheatsheets, comments, replies, DMs, the
profile, proposals, the newsletter if one ever exists.

This is the document that gets broken most often and costs the most when it is. She can smell
machine writing and she says so.

---

## 1 · The test that outranks every rule below

> If it sounds like a woman who has been in the room and has nothing left to prove, it is right.

If you cannot hear her saying it across a table, it is wrong. **Read every draft aloud in her
cadence before it goes anywhere.** This is not optional and it catches more than the checklist
does.

---

## 2 · The rhythm

Long, short. Or long, long, short. **The short line carries the meaning.**

> Often nobody is lying. That is what makes it dangerous.

Twelve words, then six. That two beat shape is the single most portable thing she does and it is
the backbone of nearly everything she writes.

**One idea per line. Blank line between lines.** Every piece ends on a line shorter than the one
before it.

---

## 3 · How she opens

Six openers, best first.

1. **The quarter turn.** Take the subject's noun and give it a different verb
2. **The declined obvious.** Name the expected response, refuse it, go elsewhere
   > I am less interested in telling CEOs to sleep more. They know.
3. **The fence.** State what a thing proves before anyone builds on it
4. **The witness.** Place her in a room like the one described, in passing
5. **The object.** Open on a physical thing
6. **The plain agreement, then the turn.** Four words maximum before the turn

**Never open with** her credentials, a statistic used as a hook, "As someone who", "This reminds
me of", a rhetorical question, a greeting by name, or a restatement of the thing she is responding
to.

---

## 4 · How she lands

All of these are hers, taken from her published posts.

> It should fit who you are now.
> Someone who can afford not to.
> Wisdom is knowing which one you are looking at.
> Capability is not consent.
> Information is not the same as truth.
> Competence is not calling. Sunk cost is not commitment.
> I can be advised. I cannot be authored.
> Progress has a human bottleneck. Us.

Under twelve words. Turns back to the reader's life, never back to the topic. Does not summarise.
Does not resolve. **She undercuts the crescendo rather than building one.**

**The negative definition is her most distinctive move.** X is not Y, as a landing, once. In the
middle of a piece it becomes connective tissue and reads as machine written. She will spot it.

**Never land on** a question to the reader, an offer, a link, a thank you, a moral, a lesson, a
summary, or an emoji.

## 5 · Her words

### The judgement set, use freely

judgement · consequential · formidable · accomplished · high performers · successor · succession ·
authority · identity · relationship · capacity · longing · pressure · conditions ·
an independent mind · a grown up question · the better moment · who you are now

**Her category noun is high performers.** Never leaders, never executives, never clients as a
label.

### Her hedges, which signal judgement and not doubt

rather · perhaps · probably · I suspect · I find this · I would ask · I would hesitate ·
I am less interested in

Counterintuitive but important. On a call she never hedges. In writing the hedge is a mark of
authority: a person who is certain does not need to announce it. **One per piece.**

### Her physical objects, use instead of abstractions

the proficiency prison · gravel and gold · a map before you enter the woods ·
the roots not the fertiliser · the steel walls and the ice · the waiting room of the railway
station · the yellow highlighter · the rectangular sky · the two blue ticks ·
the Gulfstream not the jumbo

**If a piece needs an object she has not given us, ask her for one rather than inventing it.** She
produces them instantly in conversation. This is open decision O5.

### Her abstractions with manners

She gives ideas bodies, habits and furniture.

> Questions without an alarm bell wait politely in the corridor.
> One rather inconvenient question sits quietly at the table.
> The urgent has a nasty habit of impersonating the important.
> The higher you rise, the more truth arrives pre-edited.

**One per piece. Never two.** Two is a performance.

### British spelling, always

judgement · recognise · organisations · behaviour · programme · individualising · favour ·
analyse · centre

## 6 · Banned, and it is a hard list

### The standing list

hustle · scale · crush it · hack · game changer · unlock · unlock your potential · level up ·
10x · quick win · guru · life coach · **coach** · **coaching** · holding space · journey ·
dive deep · unleash · harness · leverage · empower · transformative · elevate · curated ·
seamless · best self · authentic self · showing up · limiting beliefs · abundance · manifest ·
breakthrough as a promise · synergy · stakeholder · bandwidth · deliverable · KPI · roadmap ·
ecosystem · **midlife** · **retirement** · resilient as a recommendation · human expert ·
mindset as the starting point

In her own words she also bans *wellness clichés, inflated inspiration, corporate jargon,
superficial calendar wisdom.* And: *we are not loud, it cannot be cheap or screaming.*

### The softness trap

**She is never asking anyone to want less.**

> I did not give up ambition. I gave up self abandonment.

**Never write** slow down · do less · take it easy · balance · work life balance · self care ·
recharge · boundaries as wellness advice.

### State words, never aimed at a reader

empty · numb · caged · stuck · isolated · lonely · burnt out · exhausted

The condition may be named in the abstract and in the third person. The reader is never told what
she is. **Test: would a board member reading this embarrass the person it describes?**

### Openers banned outright

Great post · Love this · So true · Spot on · Couldn't agree more · This resonates ·
Thanks for sharing · Well said · Beautifully put · Powerful · Adding to this · Building on this ·
To piggyback · 100% · Absolutely · Exactly this · This is why I do what I do · Agreed, and I'd add

## 7 · Formatting law, no exceptions

| Banned | Note |
|---|---|
| Em dashes and en dashes | Anywhere, in any asset. The single most common tell |
| Emoji | Of any kind, including the professional ones |
| Exclamation marks | Outside quoted speech |
| Hashtags | Any number, any placement |
| Bold, italics, ALL CAPS, Unicode text tricks | In published copy. Internal documents like this one may use them |
| Bullet characters in a post | Line breaks carry the structure instead |
| Trailing ellipsis | |
| Links inside a post body | The source link goes in her own first comment |
| **A list of three** | Not three adjectives, not three parallel clauses, not three bullets opening the same way. The clearest machine tell in the material and she catches it instantly |

## 8 · The rations

More than one of any of these in a single piece is a tic.

| Move | Limit per piece |
|---|---|
| Negative definition, as a landing only | 1 |
| Witness line, "I have spent years around..." | 1 |
| Physical object, or an abstraction with manners | 1 |
| Wit beat | 1, or none |
| Signature line | 1 |
| Hedge | 1 |

**Across a week:** never the same signature line twice, and never the same object twice.

## 9 · Five things that break a draft outright

1. **It competes instead of adding.** She is the guide, never the hero.
2. **It flatters.** No compliment to the reader or the room. Her buyer has been flattered for
   thirty years and trusts none of it. Delete the flattery and see whether anything is left.
3. **It names a state at somebody.** See section 6.
4. **It praises vulnerability.** On record: she advises high performers against displaying
   vulnerability at work. Never brave, never courageous, never "thank you for being so open".
   > I don't celebrate vulnerability. If I tell one CEO, one high performer, to display
   > vulnerability, you're toast. You can show vulnerability in a safe place, but never in your
   > work environment.
5. **It sells where selling was not earned.** See doc 13 for where the offer is allowed to appear.

## 10 · A worked contrast

**The subject:** research on CEO stress levels.

> **Wrong.** "Staggering numbers. This is why prioritisation, delegation and self care are
> non-negotiable for leaders in 2026. You cannot pour from an empty cup."

A list of three. Self care, which inverts her whole position. A borrowed cliché. And it tells
powerful people something they already know.

> **Right.** "What interests me is not the stress level. They know about that.
>
> It is what pressure does to judgement. Under enough of it, the loudest problem wins and the
> expensive one waits politely in the corridor.
>
> Nothing about that shows up in an inbox."

The declined obvious as the opener. One abstraction with manners. A landing that is shorter than
the line before it and does not resolve.

---

## 11 · The hard case

A post or a message about a death, an illness or a loss.

**Default until she rules:** under fifteen words. No frame, no wisdom, no equation, no object, no
turn. **Her craft is switched off.** Sounding like a person matters more than sounding like
Nicole.

Flag it for her ruling rather than deciding it silently. This is open decision O3.
