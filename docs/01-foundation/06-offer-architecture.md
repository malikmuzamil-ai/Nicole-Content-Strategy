## The Assess, in detail

**What it is.** Half a day, private. It maps the situation and shows whether the six month work
makes sense. It is a real piece of work with its own value, not a sales call with a price tag.

**What it is not.** A discovery call. A chemistry session. A free consultation. It is paid, and
the fact that it is paid is the first filter.

**What the client leaves with.** The map. Whether or not she continues.

**Why it is the front door.** Because the belief test in doc 02 cannot be run over email, and
because somebody who will not pay CHF 4,500 to be asked the right question will not do the six
months.

## What's Next, Done Right

**Six months. Private. The North Star Circle method.**

Five acts: **Assess, Attune, Aspire, Align, Ascend.** Not a workshop weekend. Walked through on
real decisions as they actually arrive.

**The deliverable is a life operating system**, not a vision. See doc 01. The distinction is the
whole offer:

> Anyone can produce a vision in a workshop. Almost nobody survives the first real pressure test
> after.

**Built so the next decision does not need her in the room.** That line is also the answer to the
dependency objection, and it is one of the strongest Teach Me topics we hold.

## What is downstream, and not yet public

The book, the keynotes, and the Academy. All draw on the same twenty five years and the same
method.

**None of it is referenced publicly until decision O6 is settled.** A post that hints at cohorts,
a programme, or group work describes a business that does not exist yet. This is watchpoint 07 in
doc 21 and it is the failure mode most likely to happen by accident, in a single enthusiastic
sentence.

## Where the offer is allowed to appear

Exactly three places. Nowhere else.

1. The **Featured section** of her profile
2. The **bio link**
3. **Her own first comment** on a post, and only when that post is a Selling intent post that has
   earned it

**A Selling post never runs without two Authority or Education posts directly ahead of it.** The
Assess is always earned, never a cold ask.

**Zero Selling intent posts in the first thirty days.** The first application facing line does not
appear until month two.

---

## Pricing language

When the price is stated, it is stated plainly and once. No justification paragraph, no anchoring
against a competitor, no "investment" as a euphemism for price.

> The Assess is half a day, CHF 4,500.
> The six months is CHF 18,000, paid as 6,000 to begin and four payments of 3,000.

If the reaction is that it is a lot: it is. The decision underneath it is usually worth several
multiples of that, and it stops with her. That is the whole answer. Do not add to it.

---

## What we do not sell

- Frameworks, as products
- Anything self serve
- Group programmes, until O6 is settled
- Retainers, ongoing advisory, or "access"
- Discounts of any kind. A discount at this altitude reads as a reassessment of the buyer

## If O1 moves to CHF 24,000

Three things change and they change together. Do not move one without the others.

1. The ninety day revenue target moves from CHF 72,000 to CHF 96,000 at four clients
2. The Assess to client assumption should be re-examined, since a higher price usually lowers it
3. Any published or proposal language stating the current number must be swept the same week

Nothing in the content needs to change. No post states a price.
