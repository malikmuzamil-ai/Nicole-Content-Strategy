# 02 · Ideal Client Profile

**Status:** SETTLED, 11 August positioning brief
**Used by:** every post brief, the Assess application, the target list in doc 18

## The origin of the profile

> "Your target group is the person you were before transformation. So that's me. Totally me."
> Nicole

That is the most useful sentence in this document. When a draft is uncertain, the question is not
*would this resonate with founders.* It is **would this have reached her, before.**

---

## Who this is actually for

Four tests. All four have to be true.

1. **She owns or controls the business,** and the consequences of the decision stop with her.
2. **Something consequential is in front of her in the next six months.** A sale, a handover, a
   succession, an exit, a decision she has been carrying alone.
3. **She will say the true thing out loud,** including the parts she has said to nobody.
4. **She believes a different future is actually available to her,** not just to other people.

Mostly women. Founders, owner-CEOs, successors and second generation owners. Swiss and
German speaking Europe first, though the content does not geo-restrict itself.

---

## Who gets turned away

**The person who does not believe a different future is available at all.** No leaps, no invisible
forces, success only ever incremental.

That belief test belongs in the offer, and it quietly runs through the content too, since content
is what teaches a reader to believe the leap is possible before she ever applies.

Also turned away, for cleanliness:

- Anyone looking for a coach, in the sense they mean it
- Anyone wanting a framework they can run without her
- Anyone whose consequential decision is more than a year out. The urgency is the qualifier
- Anyone whose real problem is the business rather than themselves

---

## The trap, in her own structure

She has everything on the left. She has none of the right. Nobody has ever asked her about the
right hand column.

| They have | They do not have |
|---|---|
| Success | Fulfilment |
| Performance | Feeling alive |
| A role | Their real identity |
| Hustle, safety, wealth | Their calling |
| People all around them | Anyone who actually knows them |

**This table is the single most saveable asset in the whole plan.** It opens the first thirty days
as a cheatsheet, reframed narratively rather than as a listicle.

---

## The pain, and what it is the absence of

The left column is what she would say if pressed. The right column is what is actually missing.
Content names the right column, which is why it lands as recognition rather than as diagnosis.

| Pain | Absence of |
|---|---|
| Empty | Meaning |
| Numb | Aliveness |
| Caged | Inner freedom |
| Isolated | Connection |

**Hard rule.** These four words on the left are never said *at* a reader. Never "you are empty",
never "you feel numb", never "so many leaders are stuck". The condition may be named in the
abstract and in the third person. The reader is never told what she is.

The test: **would a board member reading this embarrass the person it describes?** If yes, it does
not go.

---

## What she has already tried

This matters because it is the source of one of the strongest Teach Me topics we hold.

A founder once told Nicole she had already seen a coach, a consultant and a therapist before she
called. Three different rooms, three different vocabularies, and the one question that mattered
never got asked in any of them.

Practical consequence: the reader is not uninformed. She is over-served and under-asked. Content
never explains something to her that she already knows. It asks the question nobody asked.

---

## What she is quietly waiting for

Permission. See doc 01. She has no access to her own abundance, and nobody in her professional
life is positioned to grant it.

---

## The trust factors at this altitude

These are what an accomplished person actually uses to choose an advisor, distinct from
credentials or price. Documented in her own client profile and cross checked against
executive coaching trust research.

- Somebody who **has actually been in the room** the reader is describing
- **Discretion under real stakes,** demonstrated rather than claimed
- **A presence that does not flinch** at what surfaces
- No need for the credit, and no performance of expertise

Every one of those is a writing instruction as much as a positioning note. The witness line, used
once and in passing, does more than any credential list.

---

## How to use this document when drafting

Before a post brief is approved, answer these three in one line each.

1. **Which of the four tests does this reader pass** for this post to be aimed at her
2. **Which row of the trap table** or which row of the pain table is this post touching
3. **Where is the permission,** and if there is none, why the post still earns its place

If question three has no answer twice in one week, the week is out of balance and the calendar
needs rebalancing toward Understand Me and Teach Me.
