# 07 · Claims and Confidentiality Charter

**Status:** SETTLED. This is the document with no grey area and no exceptions.
**Applies to:** every public asset, every draft, every comment, every proposal, every internal
document that might be quoted outward.

The same Confidentiality Charter that governs the private engagement governs what content can
borrow from it.

---

## Rule one: the confirmed credential list is closed

These are the only credentials that may appear in any asset. If something is not on this list, it
does not go out, even on a day a post would clearly be stronger with it.

| Confirmed | How it may be used |
|---|---|
| The **Halifax crisis response** | A room she has stood in. Never a case study, never a client claim |
| **Head of Marketing at a Swiss private bank**, including the turnaround | Operating credibility. **The bank stays unnamed** |
| **Twenty five years** around people with considerable power | The witness line |
| The **seventeen year** timeline | As stated, no elaboration |
| **Years studying Taoism** | The register and the long view. Never as a belief system, never as spirituality |
| **Training in existential psychology** | The line separating her from the wellbeing category |

**Nothing beyond that.** No client names. No company names. No awards, no memberships, no
follower counts, no "trusted by", no "worked with over X leaders", no implied roster.

Adding a credential requires her direct confirmation, in writing, logged in doc 02.

---

## Rule two: no client detail that could identify anyone

**Composite and anonymised only.** And only with her sign off, every time, no exceptions for a
stronger story.

A detail is identifying if it identifies somebody **in combination**, not only on its own. The
test is not "did we name them". The test is:

> Could a person who knows this individual read the post and know who it is?

Four ordinary facts together, a sector, a country, a deal size and a family structure, identify
somebody as reliably as a name does. Change the combination, not just the name.

**Specifically banned**
- Any named client, past or present, in any format
- Sector plus size plus location plus timing, in combination
- Deal values, even rounded
- Anything about a named public person's private life or family
- Speculation about what happened inside a named family firm, positively or negatively
- A story that only one person could possibly be

**Permitted**
- Genuinely composite figures, drawn from more than one situation
- Publicly reported facts about public figures, used as a general pattern rather than a named case
- Her own experience, within the confirmed list above

## Rule three: sourcing

Every proper noun, number, date and quote traces to her material or to a named public source.

| Kind of claim | Requirement |
|---|---|
| A statistic | Named source, and it goes in her own first comment, never in the post body |
| A quote from a public figure | Publicly reported, attributed generally, used as pattern |
| Research | Institution named. McKinsey, BCG, UBS, Harvard Business Review, Nature, the Family Business Institute |
| Anything from a call with her | Dated in the brief. "11 August positioning brief" |
| Anything else | Does not go |

**The fence rule.** State what a source actually proves before anyone builds on it. Granting first
and then narrowing the claim is one of her strongest moves and it is also what keeps us honest.

---

## Rule four: nothing publishes without her yes

Draft, then the log, then Nicole, then publish.

**There is no standing approval.** Until she grants one, every post and every comment is drafted,
batched and held. This is open decision O2.

**No DM goes out under her name before her approval.** Decided 15 September. Not negotiable, and
not covered by any standing approval that may later be granted for comments.

---

## Rule five: the three lanes

| Lane | What it covers | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | A type and a target she has already approved, using only cleared material | Goes out. Logged after |
| **Amber** | A new type, a new kind of target, a first use of one of her lines, or anything the desk is unsure about | Held. Goes to her in the batch. Never placed live |
| **Red** | Any DM, anything touching a client, anything about a named person, anything on a death or illness, anything that could read as a stance | **Stops.** Goes to her directly, named as red, before anything is written |

Until O2 is granted, **everything is amber.**

---

## Rule six: no stance

Nothing political, national, religious, or on a live controversy. No exceptions, in posts and in
comments alike. Not a hedge, not a "both sides", not a carefully worded observation. No stance,
ever.

---

## Rule seven: competitors

A competitor's post is never engaged with, positively or negatively. Not done, in either
direction. Named competitors do not appear in any asset.

Modern Elder Academy is tracked as a positioning risk in doc 21. It is never mentioned publicly.

---

## The escalation, when something is borderline

Do not resolve it quietly and do not resolve it under time pressure.

1. Mark the item **red** in the desk log
2. Write one line naming exactly what is uncertain
3. Send it to her directly, outside the batch, named as red
4. **Publish nothing in that slot.** A missed slot costs one day. A breach costs the position

A week with two missed slots and no breaches is a good week.
