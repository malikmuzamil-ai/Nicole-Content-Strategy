# 03 · Messaging House

**Status:** DRAFT. The claims are all traced to settled material. The arrangement is ours and
needs her eye once.
**Purpose:** the reusable sentences. When a post, a profile line, a proposal or a reply needs a
claim, it comes from here rather than being reinvented.

## The roof: one sentence

> They have achieved everything. They have not achieved themselves.

Everything below holds that sentence up.

## The four supporting messages

Each one maps to a pillar, so the house and the content plan are the same structure seen from two
angles.

### M1 · The trap is built from virtues, not faults

**Maps to:** Understand Me
**The claim:** extreme responsibility, a high threshold for pain, total loyalty. Every one of them
a virtue. Together they build the trap. Nobody imprisons them. Their own best qualities do it, one
reliable decision at a time.

**Why it works:** it is the only diagnosis that does not insult the reader. She has been praised
for all three her whole career. Naming them as the mechanism is new information about a life she
thought she understood.

**Reusable lines**
- Nothing is broken. That is exactly what makes it impossible to explain to anyone who could fix a broken thing.
- The skill that built the company is usually the exact one now quietly running it into a wall.
- No complaint and no inspiration. It never gets taken seriously because it does not look like a crisis.

### M2 · Success and fulfilment are not the same purchase

**Maps to:** Make Me Think
**The claim:** four pairs of words that look like synonyms and are not. Success and fulfilment.
Performance and feeling alive. A role and a real identity. People all around you and anyone who
actually knows you.

**Why it works:** it is checkable. The reader runs the test on herself inside two seconds, which is
the only reason a provocation earns its place.

**Reusable lines**
- Most people play not to lose, rather than playing to win.
- Competence is not calling.
- Everyone worth knowing knows nothing about me.

### M3 · A life operating system survives what a vision statement does not

**Maps to:** Teach Me
**The claim:** anyone can produce a vision in a workshop. Almost nobody survives the first real
pressure test after. What she builds is the version that holds when the storm arrives. Five acts:
Assess, Attune, Aspire, Align, Ascend.

**Why it works:** it converts a soft promise into a structure, without selling the structure. She
does not sell frameworks, but she is willing to show the method working on one real anonymised
decision.

**Reusable lines**
- Separate the gravel from the gold. What still belongs to you, and what you have only carried out of habit.
- Built so the next decision does not need her in the room.
- Two years from now, could you actually explain why you decided this?

### M4 · She has been in the room

**Maps to:** Entertain Me
**The claim:** twenty five years around people with considerable power. Field notes from rooms
most people only see from the outside. The pattern repeats, no matter which room it is.

**Why it works:** it is the only claim no competitor can copy, and it is the one the trust factors
in doc 02 say actually decides the hire.

**Reusable lines**
- I have spent years around people with considerable power.
- Often nobody is lying. That is what makes it dangerous.
- The higher you rise, the more truth arrives pre-edited.

### Struck on 21 September, and why

Two rows came off this floor in the evidence check. Neither may be used again in any asset.

| Struck | Why |
|---|---|
| Only 30 percent of family businesses survive to the second generation | The citation trail ends at a consultancy page that no longer resolves. The underlying 1987 study looked at 200 Illinois manufacturers and found 13 percent lasting *through* three generations, a claim repetition turned into *to*. Survival was defined as staying independent under the same name, so a good sale counted as a failure. It cannot meet doc 07 rule three |
| 168,000 Swiss SMEs by 2030, fewer than one in three with a plan | No named source. The Swiss figure that does have one is 90,667, above. The "fewer than one in three" half is worse than unsourced, it is probably wrong: Deloitte Private found 89 percent of families report having a plan |

### Candidate, and it needs her written yes before it stands on anything

The Deloitte finding reframes the whole argument and it is the better one. **The gap is not between
having a plan and not having one. Almost everyone has a plan. The gap is between a plan and a plan
that works.** 89 percent report having one, roughly half of those are thorough, 1,587 family firms,
Deloitte Private, July 2026. It is the same shape as the trap table in doc 02: everything on the
left, nothing on the right, and nobody has asked about the right hand column.

Also held for her ruling: McKinsey, February 2026, 200 listed family businesses across forty plus
countries, total shareholder return down an average 5.7 points in the five years after a family CEO
transition, with only about a third of transitions creating value.

Until she says yes in writing, logged in doc 02, neither enters the floor and no post is drafted
against either.

> Positive psychology is a good attitude. She was trained in the four last things.

That single line separates her from the entire wellbeing category in one breath. It is one of the
most valuable sentences we hold. Use it sparingly.

## Objection handling

Six objections that will arrive, and the answer in her register. These are for calls, replies and
the Assess, not for posts. A post never argues.

| Objection | The answer |
|---|---|
| "I already have a coach" | A coach hands the question back. That is the job, done properly. This is a different job |
| "I do not have time for six months" | The six months is not time added. It is the decision you are already making, made once rather than four times |
| "Is this therapy" | Therapy works through the past. This works forwards and upwards |
| "Can you guarantee an outcome" | No. I can tell you what I have seen, and I can tell you the truth while you decide |
| "Being visible online makes me look less exclusive" | Nobody applies to something they have never heard of |
| "CHF 18,000 is a lot" | It is. The decision underneath it is usually worth several multiples of that, and it stops with you |

## The softness trap

The fastest way to break the whole house. **She is never asking anyone to want less.**

> I did not give up ambition. I gave up self abandonment.

Never write: slow down, do less, take it easy, balance, work life balance, self care, recharge,
or boundaries as wellness advice. **Her offer is a larger success, not a smaller life.** A single
sentence in this direction undoes the positioning faster than a month of good posts builds it.
