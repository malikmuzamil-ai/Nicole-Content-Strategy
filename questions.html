Nicole, the questions that are actually still open.

Sixteen questions, checked first against your own documents so that nothing is put to you twice. Two that were on the earlier sheet are already settled in writing and sit at the bottom as confirmations. Under every question is what we do if you say nothing, which means silence costs a default rather than a delay. You can type straight into this page.

Four of them change what happens this week

They are marked in the warmer colour. The rest can wait without anything stopping. The first has been open since week one and it is holding the whole funnel.

The four

Each of these is currently stopping work that is otherwise finished and waiting.

01The profile rewrite. Sign it off, or mark what you want changed.

Every post drives to your profile and asks for nothing else. Three of the four ways a reader can reach the page sit on it. The rewrite is drafted and has been waiting since week one, which means the posts that have gone out are pointing at the old version.

If you say nothingThe rewrite stays in the drawer and readers keep landing on the current profile.

02What may go out under your name from today. Likes. Approved comment lines. Nothing at all.

Everything is drafted and held, which is the safe version and the slow one. Until this is settled the half hour around each post runs at a fraction of its speed.

If you say nothingNothing goes out. We keep drafting and holding.

03Is there a paid room at all, as a tier below the Assess?

Your offer document settles two tiers and this would be a third. That is a change to a settled document, so it is yours to make and not ours. Everything from week three onward assumes it exists.

If you say nothingNo room. The funnel ends at the diagnostic and the November date goes.

04If it exists: the date, the cap, the price, the language, and whether it is a room in Zurich or online.

Five settings, and the registration page, the payment product and every letter in the campaign hang on them. The cap is the number you can defend out loud, set by your own capacity rather than by a revenue target.

If you say nothingNothing is built and Thursday 5 November is released.

Open, and each one holds something up

None of these stops the build outright. Each one costs speed, or costs us a slot.

05Who should we be engaging with? Names if you have them, and anyone you want left alone.

Four lists have been built and they are thinner than they should be. Of seventy accounts on the first, ten or twelve post more than once a week. Your buyers are owners, and owners do not post much. Twenty names from you are worth more than four hundred from a search. A list of the rooms or the sectors you want watched works just as well if names are easier to give later.

If you say nothingWe keep building from sector and title, and the list stays thinner than it should be.

06The diagnostic needs a title. Three of your own lines are coming to you. Choose one.

Your own standard is that a title is a scene or a question and never a promise. We would rather hand you three things you have already said than name it ourselves.

If you say nothingThe page is built and sits unpublished until it has a name.

07Three or four of your own physical objects, for the image bank.

The proficiency prison, gravel and gold, the rectangular sky. You produce these in conversation in seconds and we are not going to invent one.

If you say nothingAny post that needs an object moves down the calendar rather than going out with a borrowed one.

08A post about a death, an illness or a loss. What do we do underneath it?

This is one of the likeliest live situations and the one where craft is the wrong instinct. It will arrive on a day nobody planned for it.

If you say nothingUnder fifteen words, no frame and no turn, flagged to you before it goes.

09A comment arrives in German. Reply in German, or send it to you?

We can draft in either. What we cannot do is guess at your tone in the second one, and tone in a second language is exactly where a voice slips.

If you say nothingWe draft the German reply and send it to you first. Nothing in German goes out unread.

10The advisers around your buyer. Lawyers, fiduciaries, bankers. In or out?

They post far more than owners do and they refer. They are also not the buyer, and engaging them dilutes what the list is for.

If you say nothingA separate, smaller list. Engaged less often, counted apart, so we can see what it is worth.

11Some sentence shapes repeat across your posts. Flagged with one alternative beside your own version, or left alone?

We change nothing either way. The repetition is load bearing and anyone who edits it out removes the thing that makes it yours. You would simply see what a sharp reader sees.

If you say nothingWe flag on the first post of each week. You ignore us as often as you like.

12Two succession numbers came off your proof floor this week. May the replacements go on?

The thirty percent survival figure cannot be traced to a real study, and the claim that fewer than one in three owners has a plan is contradicted by Deloitte, who found that almost all of them do. What replaces them is named and dated, and the argument gets better: the gap is not between having a plan and not having one, it is between a plan and a plan that works.

If you say nothingSuccession posts run without a number, and week three of the calendar publishes without one.

Your funnel document settles three places the offer may appear and the letter is not one of them. The reference we want to put there points at the diagnostic rather than at the Assess, which is arguably outside that rule, but reading a settled rule generously is not something we should do on our own.

If you say nothingThe first issue goes out with no link in it.
