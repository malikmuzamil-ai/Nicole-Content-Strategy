Rebuilt against the confirmed positioning, in her own visual identity. Every pillar carries the topics that actually belong to it, and the first thirty days are planned post by post.

Private6 sectionsFinalThe ninety day build, 21 September

They have achieved everything. They have not achieved themselves.

That sentence is the whole thesis. Content exists to say it in a hundred different ways until the right reader recognises herself in it.

Strategic North Star

Every post positions Nicole as the advisor who brings accomplished people back to themselves, so they can do justice to their own greatness. Every post fits one of four pillars, built around what it does to the reader, not the topic underneath it. Content is not the goal. It is the unpaid channel that turns a stranger into an application.

Core Positioning Statement

For accomplished founders and owner-CEOs who have built everything except themselves, Nicole Brandes brings them back to who they actually are, so they can finally do justice to their own greatness.

Not a coach. Not a consultant. Not a therapist.

What she actually delivers

Not a vision. A life operating system. Anyone can produce a vision in a workshop. Almost nobody survives the first real pressure test after. A life operating system is the version that holds when the storm arrives.

"Being true to yourself gives you incredible strength. Not strength from status or prestige. Those will not endure when you lose a child."Nicole

The mechanism underneath it: permission

"People need permission for that. They have no access to their own abundance."Nicole

Identity is what changes. Permission is what lets it change. A coach hands the question back, so no permission is ever granted. This is the specific thing content has to give a reader before she will ever apply anywhere: the sense that someone has just given her permission for the thing she already knew.

The market is on a deadline. Just over ninety thousand Swiss SMEs need a succession arrangement in the coming years, and roughly one in three disappears because it never finds a purchaser. The gap is not between having a plan and not having one. Almost everyone has a plan. The gap is between a plan and a plan that works, and nobody has ever asked them about the second one.

The platform rewards exactly this kind of writing. LinkedIn now favours how long someone actually stays on a post, saves, and real comments, over quick reactions. Nicole is one of the few people in this category willing to write like this.

Everything downstream, the book, the keynotes, the Academy being quietly built behind the scenes, draws on the same twenty five years and the same method. Content is simply the first place people get to see it.

Who this is for, and the four pillars she already named

These are not our pillars. They are Nicole's own words, kept exactly as she said them, given a weight and a job.

"Your target group is the person you were before transformation. So that's me. Totally me."Nicole

Who this is actually for

  • She owns or controls the business, and the consequences of the decision stop with her.
  • Something consequential is in front of her in the next six months.
  • She will say the true thing out loud, including the parts she has said to nobody.
  • She believes a different future is actually available to her, not just to other people.

The one she turns away: the person who does not believe a different future is available at all. No leaps, no invisible forces, success only ever incremental. That belief test belongs in the offer, and it quietly runs through the content too, since content is what teaches a reader to believe the leap is possible before she ever applies.

Given up on purpose

  • Bait and engagement pods. The platform pushes both down anyway, and they would cheapen the account even if it didn't.
  • Writing that sounds AI-generated. Every draft is read aloud in her own cadence before it goes up.
  • The standard case-study format. Before, after, logo, quote. The category she is leaving behind.
  • Any client detail that could identify someone, even indirectly. Composite and anonymised only, and only with her sign-off, every time, no exceptions for a stronger story. The same Confidentiality Charter that governs the private engagement governs what content can borrow from it.
  • Any credential not already confirmed directly by her. Halifax, the private bank turnaround, the twenty five and seventeen year timeline, the years of Taoist study, and the training in existential psychology. Nothing beyond that.
  • Heavy personal storytelling. She said it herself, she is reserved about her own stories. Content stays built on the questions, not on mining her life for material.

What this is really up against

Not a competitor. A belief worth naming out loud: that being visible online makes someone at this level look less exclusive, not more. Nobody applies to something they have never heard of. Staying quiet is the actual risk, not the writing.

A Selling post never runs without two Authority or Education posts directly ahead of it. The Assess is always earned, never a cold ask.

What we are watching

Written the way we would want it written for ourselves. Nothing here is fatal. All of it is cheaper to fix now than in month four.

01Too much volume, not enough substance

Three posts a week is the ceiling, not a floor to defend by cutting corners. Her own words: "we can't just be a content machine."

02The paradox line stops landing

It works because it is rare. Weekly instead of twice a month, it stops feeling like a punch in the belly.

03A credential or client detail slips in early

Every unconfirmed detail, and every client detail specific enough to identify someone, stays out until Nicole clears it herself directly, even on a day a post would clearly be stronger with it.

04Someone else claims this ground first

Modern Elder Academy has a real audience and a proven playbook. Showing up consistently now is what keeps this ground.

05Staying classy against needing reach

Not loud, not cheap, set against the volume needed to reach twelve Assess sessions. Depth over reach is the right call at this price, but it should stay a choice, not an accident.

06Advisor, coach, and the line between

Settled as how she is described publicly, not as who she is privately. The word coach never appears in public.

07Content gets ahead of the offer

If a post starts hinting at cohorts or group programs before that side of the business is ready, it describes a business that does not exist yet.

The one that matters most

There is no content plan here without Nicole in the room after it. Every application still has to be met by one person, showing up. The job of content is to bring in the right four clients, not the most applicants.
